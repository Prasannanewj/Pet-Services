# Product Vision and OKRs

## Vision Statement

Make ongoing pet care easier and more rewarding by connecting owners, veterinary clinics, and trusted support at the moments that matter.

The experience helps owners manage prescribed medications and veterinarian-recommended vaccinations while offering relevant, optional education and promotions. Veterinarians remain responsible for clinical recommendations; owners control their data and marketing preferences.

## Future-State Journey

| Moment | Owner experience | Clinic and business experience |
|---|---|---|
| At the clinic | The veterinarian discusses a care plan; the owner receives an invitation. | Staff introduce the program using a short workflow. |
| Set up | The owner adds a pet, instructions, clinic contact, and vaccination dates from records; chooses reminder and marketing preferences separately. | Enrollment and consent are recorded without requiring a replacement clinical system. |
| Build a routine | The owner receives selected reminders, records what happened, and invites a caregiver. | The clinic encourages use without monitoring a new inbox. |
| Receive support | The owner sees reviewed education and, if opted in, relevant eligible offers. | Marketing uses approved campaigns and consented channels. |
| Prepare for vaccination | A reminder reflects a recorded or clinic-provided date; the owner contacts the clinic to confirm what is appropriate. | The clinic schedules and records vaccination in its existing workflow. |
| Redeem and return | The owner saves an eligible digital coupon and views loyalty benefits and terms. | Redemption supports campaign measurement; offers do not determine treatment. |
| Review care | The owner records observations and chooses whether to share a summary. | The veterinarian receives self-reported context, not a diagnosis or verified adherence. |

The product does not prescribe, adjust medication, determine vaccination eligibility, or provide emergency triage. Logs are not monitored; owners should contact their veterinary team for concerns.

## Strategic Differentiators

1. **Care-first engagement:** The core experience remains useful without promotions.
2. **Clinic-connected, not clinic-dependent:** Clinics can introduce it without replacing existing systems.
3. **One coordinated journey:** Medication, vaccination reminders, education, coupons, and loyalty connect around owner preferences.
4. **Household-friendly design:** Caregivers see the latest recorded status and recorder.
5. **Transparent personalization:** Owners understand offer relevance and can change preferences or opt out.
6. **Trusted AI:** AI supports discovery and organization of approved information, not clinical decisions.

## Proposed 12-Month OKRs

Targets are planning hypotheses, not established baselines. Confirm them after discovery and pilot measurement.

### O1. Make ongoing care easier and sustain owner engagement

| Key result | Year-one target |
|---|---|
| Enrolled households completing medication setup and recording one dose within seven days | 60% |
| Activated households completing a care-support action in week four | 40% |
| Paired survey respondents reporting easier medication management at week four | 50% |
| Inviting households with a second caregiver viewing or recording the routine | 30% |

### O2. Increase participation in veterinarian-recommended preventive care

| Key result | Year-one target |
|---|---|
| Owners choosing vaccination support who capture a date or clinic-confirmed reminder | 50% |
| Clinic contact or booking following an eligible vaccination reminder | Establish baseline, then 10% relative lift in a controlled test |
| Completed vaccination after a reminder | Establish baseline; report verified clinic confirmations separately from owner-reported completions |

Reminders prompt a clinic conversation; they do not establish clinical eligibility.

### O3. Make clinic adoption practical and valuable

| Key result | Year-one target |
|---|---|
| Enrolled participating clinics | 100 |
| Enrolled clinics introducing the program within 30 days | 70% |
| Enrolled clinics introducing the program in the final pilot month | 60% |
| Surveyed clinic staff saying introduction is manageable | 70% |

### O4. Grow relevant, consent-based commercial engagement

| Key result | Year-one target |
|---|---|
| Enrolled owners separately opting in to marketing | Establish baseline; target 40% |
| Unique eligible offer recipients redeeming a digital coupon | 10% |
| Controlled test measuring redemption lift | At least one |
| Eligible loyalty members using at least one benefit | Establish baseline; report opt-outs and complaints |

Offers require approved terms and must not determine treatment. Loyalty benefits must not be contingent on a veterinarian prescribing a particular treatment.

### O5. Introduce trustworthy AI-driven recommendations

| Key result | Year-one target |
|---|---|
| Supervised pilot of recommendations for approved educational content | One |
| Sampled outputs passing predefined relevance and approved-content checks | 90% |
| Review, monitoring, escalation, and rollback procedures | Complete before pilot launch |

AI is limited to reviewed educational content and eligible offers. It must not diagnose, recommend medication changes, or determine vaccination schedules.

## Business Impact

| Impact | Mechanism | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Greater awareness and reach | Clinic invitations and consented omnichannel communication. | Eligible owners reached and enrollment conversion. |
| Stronger clinic participation | Low-burden support for existing workflows. | Activation, sustained participation, and staff-reported burden. |
| More effective promotions | Relevant, measurable digital offers. | Redemption and incremental lift versus control. |
| Improved engagement | Reminders, education, shared care, and loyalty. | Week-four engagement, repeat use, and opt-outs. |
| Better investment decisions | Consistent program and campaign reporting. | Cost per activated household and incremental redemption; pilot economics. |

## Customer Impact

- **Owners:** Less uncertainty about routines, refills, upcoming care, and available support.
- **Caregivers:** Better coordination and fewer handoff ambiguities.
- **Veterinarians:** More useful owner-reported context for conversations.
- **Clinic managers:** A straightforward introduction without a separate monitored inbox.
- **Marketing teams:** Clearer consent, eligibility, campaign performance, and relevance.

Reported doses and completed vaccinations are not automatically verified clinical outcomes. Health or adherence claims require appropriate evidence.

## Innovation Roadmap

| Timing | Focus | Deliverables and gate |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1-3 | Discover and design | Research, prototypes, baseline metrics, and consent, offer, safety, and data requirements; validate need and workflow feasibility. |
| Months 4-6 | Launch core pilot | Medication reminders, shared records, owner-entered vaccination reminders, clinic invitations, and basic digital coupons; confirm usability and safety readiness. |
| Months 7-9 | Personalize and learn | Opt-in education and offers, loyalty pilot, campaign controls, and reporting; check engagement, trust, and clinic burden. |
| Months 10-12 | Test AI and decide on scale | Supervised recommendations using approved content, controlled evaluation, and scale business case; review value, impact, safety, economics, and readiness. |
